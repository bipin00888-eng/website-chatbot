# About Area 77

Area 77 is Hyderabad's only experiential learning destination of its kind — a 150-acre protected reserve where agriculture, recreation, education, and adventure come together in one immersive environment. The name stands for A-R-E-A: Agriculture, Recreation, Education, Adventure. Set amid rich flora and fauna and a thriving Miyawaki afforestation zone, Area 77 offers curated, ticketed experiences that let visitors learn by doing rather than simply observe. It suits family outings, school field trips, corporate offsites, or a quiet morning walk.

Area 77 quick facts: 150 acres of protected reserve, over 50 bird species visit the property, and farm access is open 365 days a year. It is a strictly plastic-free environmental sanctuary.

## Agriculture

Agriculture is the beating heart of Area 77. Using modern farming technology, the reserve cultivates exotic produce — strawberries, avocados, cherries, and more — in open farms in Hyderabad. Activities include: farm-to-hand picking experiences (strawberry picking, sunflower picking, and seasonal fruit/vegetable picking through the year); half-day and full-day corporate and school programs; hands-on "plow, till, sow, and harvest" farming with the team; a working dairy and poultry farm; animal feeding and bird watching (50+ bird species); a working honeybee farm where visitors learn how honey is made; and a walking/running track open every day through the farm and forest.

## Recreation

The recreation zone at Area 77 is built for visitors of every age and includes: a 16-hole mini golf course, an RC car racing track, an RC car crawling track, RC boat racing on the lake, and an outdoor arcade. The entire recreation zone doubles as a venue for birthday parties and corporate retreats.

## Education

Area 77 offers structured educational programming for all age groups, blending curriculum-linked learning with hands-on nature immersion: an Advanced Learner's Program for high school students integrated with classroom study; practical lessons on farming and its benefits; introductory botanical studies on flowers, vegetables, and fruits; naturalist-led nature walks explaining the property's flora; themed gardens including the Spice Garden, Herbal Garden, Tulsi Garden, Rose Garden, and Nakshatra Garden; a Buddha Garden dedicated to quiet meditation; and an Open-Air Reading Garden for reading and unwinding.

## Adventure

For visitors seeking a physical challenge, Area 77 offers a 7-obstacle rope course (about 60 minutes to complete), cycling trails, hiking routes, trail exploring, and guided exploration of the on-site Miyawaki forest — a dense, fast-grown microforest planted using the Miyawaki method, with a noticeably different microclimate from the rest of the reserve.

## Events at Area 77

Area 77 is also a premier event space with three settings across three scales: the Lakeside Lawn & Cabana Venue, an intimate waterfront setting suited to dinners and small weddings for 150–200 guests; the Bamboo Shelter & Central Lawn, the largest venue, an open central lawn beneath a bamboo shelter for big-scale celebrations of 300–500 guests; and the Recreational Zone (RC cars, mini golf, RC boats, adventure course, cycling, animal feeding), bookable for kids' birthday parties and corporate retreats.

## Berry & Bean — the café at Area 77

Berry & Bean is Area 77's in-house specialty coffee house — a lakeside café serving its own tea and coffee blends. It has lakeside seating right along the water, and every other Area 77 activity (mini golf, RC cars and boats, cycling) is within walking distance of the table, making it a place to work, relax, or socialise between activities.
