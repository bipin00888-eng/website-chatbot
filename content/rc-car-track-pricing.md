## Track rules and terms

All track time is monitored by CCTV. Any damages caused wantedly (deliberately) to cars must be paid for in full. No deliberate bashing into other cars — violators will be voided from the track. Do not enter the racing track area on foot. No food is allowed in the playing paddock area.
