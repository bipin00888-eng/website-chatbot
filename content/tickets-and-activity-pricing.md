# Area 77 ticketing and activity pricing

These prices and tickets are offered 365 days a year. No food is provided with any ticket — all food and beverages must be purchased separately at the cafeteria/coffee shop.

## Ticket options

Coffee Explorer ticket: ₹499. Includes 1 coffee/beverage plus access to the park.

Adventure Ticket: ₹1,199. Includes the rope course (60 minutes), cycling (30/60 minutes, priced separately at ₹299/₹399), nature walks, and water stream access.

Kids Farm Day Pass: ₹699. Includes access to the fields for picking, animal feeding, sensory yard access, an art & craft station, and a kids' tractor ride (30 minutes). Produce picked can be taken home, charged separately per kg at the rate set by management.

Mini Golf: 16 holes, ₹499 for 30 minutes or ₹799 for 60 minutes, including mini golf equipment and course access.

Sunday Picnic Day: ₹1,299. Includes the day's farming activities, cycling (60 minutes), animal feeding, nature walks, a lakeside picnic with music, water stream access, the wishing well, and sensory yard access.

## Individual add-ons

Animal feeding: ₹299. Cycling: ₹299 for 30 minutes or ₹399 for 60 minutes. Kids tractor ride: ₹499.

## Activity price table (per-activity, à la carte)

Cycling: 30 or 60 minutes, ₹299 / ₹399, includes bicycle, helmet, and access to designated cycling trails.

Mini golf: 30 or 60 minutes, ₹499 / ₹799, includes mini golf equipment and course access.

Adventure (rope course activities): 1 hour, ₹999, includes adventure activities with harness provided.

Outdoor arcade: ₹299, includes sand bags, dots, balancing, balls & bucket games.

RC car: ₹999 per battery, includes the RC car and controller.

## Wrist bands

Red Band: access to adventure activities. Yellow Band: access to the coffee shop. Orange Band (Day Pass), full day: ₹1,499, includes the coffee shop, adventure activities, and sightseeing. Entry is free for coffee sippers and for those doing paid activities.
