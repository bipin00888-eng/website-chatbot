# Area 77 school & outdoor experiential learning programs

Area 77 runs an Outdoor Experiential Learning Program for schools, eco-clubs, and summer camps, redefining education through experiential learning, physical challenges, agricultural science, and artistic expression.

## Key guidelines for school management

Area 77 is a strictly plastic-free sanctuary. The recommended/required teacher-to-student supervision ratio is 1:25. Any child allergies or medical conditions must be emailed to Area 77 prior to arrival so support arrangements can be made. Catering is optional — programs can be booked with or without a meal plan. Cycling and mini-golf are add-on activities and must be specified at the time of booking.

## Grade-level structure

Primary School (Ages 4–10, focus: Sensory & Motor Skills): tactile farming, animal feeding empathy, entry-level obstacle courses, guided plant discovery, mini-golf play, and lakeside nature art.

Middle School (Ages 11–13, focus: Dynamics & Ecology): farm-to-table experience, elevated ropes obstacles, soil science, botanical cycles, cycling paths, and lakeside nature painting.

High School (Ages 14+, focus: Leadership & Resilience): advanced high-ropes navigation, strategic leadership challenges, in-depth agricultural concepts, environmental mindfulness, animal care, and creative reset sessions.

## Program core pillars

1. STEM & Ecology: farm-to-table concepts, strawberry harvests, soil health, botanical walks, ecological exploration, and ethical animal care.

2. Action & Leadership: high-energy rope courses, collaborative group problem solving, mini-golf geometry and sportsmanship, and cycling on open-air tracks.

3. Arts & Well-Being: lakeside nature canvas painting, open-air art workshops, and environmental mindfulness walks that help alleviate academic stress and reduce anxiety.

## Curriculum alignment

The program bridges textbook theory and real-world application. For IGCSE students, the Miyawaki Forest is a case study for Environmental Management and Biology (urban afforestation, carbon sinks, indigenous biodiversity). For IB students, it aligns with Environmental Systems and Societies (ESS) — analyzing the multi-layered Miyawaki forest structure for Succession and Intraspecific Competition, useful as primary data for Internal Assessments. It also supports IB CAS (Creativity, Activity, Service): Creativity via documenting forest growth or designing an "Eco-Trail" guide; Activity via rope courses and physical farming tasks; Service via participation in sustainable organic agriculture and biodiversity maintenance.

## Food & catering for school groups

Area 77 offers end-to-end dining/catering: in-house buffet services with fresh, hygienic, student-friendly menus prepared on site; customizable menu styles (high-nutrition continental spreads or traditional regional formats) to suit dietary needs; hydration hubs with continuous water/refreshment stations along activity tracks; and full staff support (dedicated setup, serving, and cleanup crews) so teachers can focus on supervision rather than logistics.

## School registration form — information needed to book

To book a school visit, Area 77's Education Program Coordinators need: school name and date of proposed visit; contact person/coordinator name, phone number and email; estimated student count and accompanying teacher count by grade level (Primary Ages 4–10, Middle Ages 11–13, High School Ages 14+); which program/package is wanted (Sample Day Excursion Schedule, Advanced Young Explorer's Program at ₹2,499, Laid-Back Experience Package, or a Custom Multi-Pillar Program); any optional add-ons (Mini-Golf Greens Tournament, Cycling & Open-Air Tracks, Pottery Wheel Workshop); catering/meal preference (full in-house buffet lunch, continental menu style, traditional regional format, no catering/program only, or special dietary/medical requirements detailed by email); and a signed declaration acknowledging the plastic-free policy and the 1:25 teacher-to-student supervision ratio. Schools should contact Area 77's Education Program Coordinators to schedule a site walkthrough, review custom menu packages, or tailor a day-out itinerary to their curriculum objectives.
